Shree Devi Institute of Technology Mangalore has successfully placed all eligible interested candidates of Engineering, MBA and MCA for the current academic year through the ongoing campus placements. In the year 2016 – 427+ offer letters were given, In the year 2017 – 452+ offer letters were given, In the year 2018 – 461+ offer letters were given, In the year 2019 – 480+ offer letters were given and In the year 2020 – 492+ offer letters were given to our students with most of the eligible students being successfully placed in various reputed companies.
The academic excellence at Shree Devi Institute Technology is reflected in its student’s exemplary record in campus placements in the corporate and engineering sectors. Shree Devi Institute Technology has consistently maintained an excellent recruitment record. The graduates and post- graduates of Shree Devi Institute Technology have been recruited by leading Indian companies.
On an average 90 – 95% of eligible final year students are placed through campus placements in the last 3 years. Placements in Shree Devi Institute of Technology begins in the seventh semester. Several efforts to keep the final year students updated on industry requirements, prepare them for enhancing their employability has been made during the year. Students have attended on campus as well as pool campus interviews and have been placed in reputed companies. This year’s placement witnessed healthy participation, ensuring plenty of opportunities for the candidates to pursue their career in the field of their preference.
Shree Institute of Technology can justifiably look back at its placement record in recent years with a sense of deep satisfaction. The students have validated the trust placed in them by the institution and recruiters as evidenced by the feedback received from the recruiters. We are encouraged to see many companies revert to us time and again for recruitment. This enhances our faith in ourselves and the academic input provided at Shree Devi Institute of Technology.
